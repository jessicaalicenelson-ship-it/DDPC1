Questions you might have

Do I need to know exactly what help I want?

Not at all. The free conversation is for figuring that out together.

Can I arrange this for someone else?

Yes. Tell us you are interested in gifting care and we'll discuss the recipient's needs, location, and schedule before making arrangements.

Are you a medical or lactation provider?

No. Postpartum doula support is non-medical and does not replace care from a physician, nurse, or qualified lactation professional.

Where do you work?

Davis, California, and nearby communities by arrangement. Travel may affect availability and pricing.
